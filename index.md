## What I'm planning

- A page for each category - listing and linking out to groups, projects and initiatives in the space. 
- An events page, listing and linking out to all the events (big and small) that I can find. 
- A 'what is open source' explainer page for my mum and my peers who don't work in tech.
- Maybe a high-level glossary. These exist elsewhere so I'm not sold on the idea but it might be useful. Let's see how we go.
- A blog with current RSS and future newsletter signup. I intend to write some stuff and it would be good to have a space to publish things written by other people.

I'm intending to reach out to people who know more than me to get valuable insight into a lot of these topics. If you are a cool person I should talk to (or know someone who is), please let me know: [hello@opensourceaustralia.guide](mailto:hello@opensourceaustralia.guide).
